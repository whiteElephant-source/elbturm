The hereditary building right (Erbbaurecht) divides property ownership by separating the land from the building. The hereditary building right grantor is the owner of the land, while the hereditary building right holder owns the buildings. With the hereditary building right contract, the landowner transfers the property to the hereditary building right holder for an agreed period of time. 

Usage 
The hereditary building right contract can specify the type of use, objectives for management, and construction activities. These objectives are binding for the entire duration of the contract. Residential, commercial, social, or cultural uses can thus be protected from displacement by future developments. Selected uses are eligible for a reduced interest rate. 

Interest 
These objectives are valid for the duration of the hereditary building right and are therefore an essential element of the contract's design. The hereditary building right interest rate is calculated based on the land value and is freely negotiable. It usually ranges from 3 to 6.5 percent annually, depending on the type of use and the objectives. 
 
Elbtower 
None of the participants of the bidding process for the plot of the Elbtower in 2018, was interested in leasing the land to the city's proposed hereditary building right. The city never insisted on its enforcement either. No surprise, the hereditary building right restricts speculation or land-banking to such a degree, that it becomes irrelevant for high yield investors.
