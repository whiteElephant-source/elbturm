Image Henry George: «Everybody works but the vacant lot»

Grindelallee 80 in Hamburg: Following half a decade of abandonment, the derelict building dating from the Gründerzeit period, located at Grindelallee 80, underwent a judicially mandated auction overseen by the Hamburg District Court. The property was acquired for the sum of €5 million by the development firm, Min to Huus. The transference of ownership resulted from the former proprietor‘s insolvency, a consequence of accruing penalties imposed by the district‘s administrative body. The previous owner committed several violations, including using forceful tactics that pushed the tenants out. The building remained vacant in the hopes to resell the commodity. By rising rents, tenants couldn‘t afford anymore, the property‘s value went up on paper. (1)
